# Benchmark notes
Product features verified from public pages; limitations below are project-fit gaps or test risks, not proven defects. No comparative returns audit.
- TradingView: https://www.tradingview.com/pine-script-docs/concepts/strategies/ — strategy simulation; learn fill modeling, guard lookahead and costs.
- TrendSpider: https://trendspider.com/ — automated charts, AI, strategy tester and alerts; benchmark India-specific coverage and independent source provenance.
- Trade Ideas: https://www.trade-ideas.com/ — scanning and AI signals; require auditable rationale, check venue coverage and data delay.
- Strike Money: https://www.strike.money/ — Indian scanners, rotation, sentiment, fundamentals; benchmark catalyst-to-filing evidence links.
- Screener: https://www.screener.in/ — India fundamentals; homepage discloses BSE 5-minute and NSE 15-minute delayed data. Not for live entries.
- Koyfin: https://www.koyfin.com/ — macro and portfolio analysis; benchmark cross-asset context and concise output.
Institutional study leads (not audited in this pass): Renaissance Technologies, DE Shaw, Two Sigma, Citadel, Jane Street, Goldman Sachs. Study public research on statistical validation, diversification, liquidity and risk; do not infer secret strategies or actual performance.
